# Asset sourcing and professional tool research

Checked 2026-11-06 against official sources linked below. No assets were bought, downloaded into the game or approved. Quality assessments are suitability judgments from documented features/listing descriptions; they are not engine validation. The supplied references remain the artistic authority.

**Verified** means the linked primary page explicitly supports the named capability/license. **Review** means the selected item, full terms, actual download, topology, budget or entitlement still needs inspection. Marketplace previews and a license summary are not a purchase contract. No generic character pack is recommended.

## Custom commissions instead of uncertain stock

Custom-model the reference-specific house shell/gables/gingerbread trim and hero kitchen fixtures, original family models, hair and clothing. Commission wallpaper motifs/cloth patterns if no legally usable source matches. Architectural trim can be a single artist-approved curve/profile library with bake/instancing automation. Functional fridge, oven, drawers, taps and doors require separate interior pieces and pivot metadata even if a stock mesh supplies the exterior.

For every acquired asset keep a manifest: exact title/vendor/URL, license version, acquisition date, receipt/attribution, source formats, texture resolution/channel conventions, modifications, mesh/skin/animation counts, bounds/units, runtime compression and owner. No pack is approved until it meets both reference style and game budgets.
